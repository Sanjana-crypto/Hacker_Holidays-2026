# 🤖 Day 01 – The Concierge Knows Too Much

## 📌 Overview

The first challenge introduces VERA, the Byte Lotus Hotel's AI concierge. The assistant appears to know guest information before any interaction, leading to an investigation into how trust, identity, and AI assistants can be abused.

This room focuses on understanding how AI systems may unintentionally expose sensitive information when proper safeguards are missing.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand how the AI assistant identifies trusted users.
- Explore prompt injection and social engineering concepts.
- Learn how trust relationships affect AI responses.
- Complete the challenge without exposing active spoilers.

## 📚 Key Learnings

- AI assistants should never blindly trust user identity.
- Prompt injection can manipulate LLM behaviour.
- Identity verification is critical in AI-powered systems.
- Sensitive instructions should never be exposed to end users.
- Security boundaries are just as important for AI as they are for traditional applications.
